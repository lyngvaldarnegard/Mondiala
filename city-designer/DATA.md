Mondiala in numbers: **397 hexagons** across rings 0–11 · **397 named hexes** · **78 pink free-use districts** (v1.8: the six favourite gods of love hold the ring-4 middles — one allocation list, the favourites at the top; the cradle goddesses wait unplaced) · **265 world cities** on the roster, **1** of them waiting in the pool.

The map is read-only in the browser — every change happens in the data files.
