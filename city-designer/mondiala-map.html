Inspector

Click a hexagon to edit it. Drag to pan, scroll to zoom — zooming out stops at the full-city view.

Brush

Paint mode: click hexes to apply the armed type. In Select mode, click a type to change the selected hex.

City stats

Hex side = 1 km → each hex ≈ 2.598 km². Population = density × area. Boulevards are 100 m wide. Rings 4–8 place the world's great cities by their real coordinates — distance from Mondiala picks the ring, bearing picks the hexagon. Ring 3 (Norden) sorts by population. Hexagon 1 is the top of each ring; numbers run clockwise and the green spoke runs straight north–south. Every non-green hexagon touches a pink free-use district.

Unplaced names

Cities beyond the map's current horizon — add a ring or more cities to reach them. Click to arm, then click a hex (Select mode) to place a name by hand.
