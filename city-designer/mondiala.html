City stats

Hex side = 1 km → each hex ≈ 2.598 km². Population = density × area. Boulevards are 100 m wide. The heart is authored by hand — ring 1 the six cradle civilisations, ring 2 the twelve ancient cities, ring 3 the Nordics with every Nordic country represented, ring 4 the inspiration cities with the six favourite gods of love on the middles. Rings 5+ place the world's great cities by real geography — the seating is decided by deterministic formulas (real km between cities, the compass of the lat/lon) and ships as generated tables, so the map opens instantly. Hexes have no coordinates — the cities on them do; the continent view colours every hex by its city's continent in the Olympic ring colours, and the pink free-use districts stay pink in every view. Hexagon 1 is the top of each ring; numbers run clockwise and the green spoke runs straight north–south. Gods of love never sit on the green spokes, the pink districts stay mirror-symmetric about the north–south axis, and every ordinary hexagon of the outer city touches a pink district.
