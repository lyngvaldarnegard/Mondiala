City stats

Hex side = 1 km → each hex ≈ 2.598 km². Population = density × area. Boulevards are 100 m wide. The heart is authored by hand — ring 1 the inspiration cities, ring 2 the cradle civilisations, ring 3 each cradle's first city plus Athens, Constantinople, Rome and Carthage, ring 4 Norden with every Nordic country represented. The empty heart hexes wait to be filled with gods of love. Rings 5–8 place the world's great cities by their real coordinates — distance from Mondiala picks the ring, bearing picks the hexagon. Hexagon 1 is the top of each ring; numbers run clockwise and the green spoke runs straight north–south. Gods of love never sit on the green spokes, the pink districts stay mirror-symmetric about the north–south axis, and every ordinary hexagon outside Norden touches a pink district.
