City stats

Hex side = 1 km → each hex ≈ 2.598 km². Population = density × area. Boulevards are 100 m wide. The heart is authored by hand — ring 1 the six cradle civilisations, ring 2 the twelve ancient cities, ring 3 Norden with every Nordic country represented. Ring 4 (v1.8): the six original inspiration cities hold the green spokes (which spoke by their true bearing), the six favourite gods of love take the triads' middles facing their homelands, and the twelve other inspiration cities keep their ring-4 seats by bearing. Rings 5 and beyond place the world's great cities by their real coordinates — bearing picks the hex, one band (v1.9): all free hexes of rings 5+ form one continuous compass band (by angle, inner ring first within an angle), the nearest distance-ranked batch of cities rides the band in bearing order in one monotone pass, so each direction stays contiguous across ALL rings (Africa south, the Americas west, Asia and Oceania east), near = inner within a direction, spokes are ordinary tiles, only the outmost ring may hold empty city tiles. Hexagon 1 is the top of each ring; numbers run clockwise and the green spoke runs straight north–south. Gods of love never sit on the green spokes, the pink districts stay mirror-symmetric about the north–south axis, and every ordinary hexagon of the outer city touches a pink district.
